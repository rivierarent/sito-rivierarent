Owner FAQ

Quick answers

How much does it cost?

We work on commission on actual bookings: the percentage depends on the home and the services included, and we confirm it in writing after the visit.

Can I use my home whenever I want?

Yes. Block your dates and the calendar closes automatically on every portal.

Do I need a CIN?

Yes, the national identification code (CIN) is mandatory for short-term rentals in Italy and must be shown in listings. We help you apply for it and handle the other requirements.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Liguria, coast and inland.
