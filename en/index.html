How key handover works

  1. You leave us the keys. Drop them at the info point with the guest's name and arrival date. You sign the key register and the keys are tagged with a code (never with the address).
  2. The guest collects them. During office hours, showing an ID document. We record name, ID, date and time in the register and the guest signs.
  3. Return. On departure the guest brings the keys back to us; the register entry is closed and we let you know on WhatsApp.
  4. Outside office hours? By appointment or via a key safe, for a surcharge.

How linen hire works

  1. Book your packs. Tell us how many people are arriving and when: one pack per person (sheets, pillowcase, towels).
  2. Leave a deposit. On your first pick-up you pay a linen deposit, recorded in the register.
  3. Pick up and return. Collect clean packs and bring back the used ones, even at the same time as you pick up new ones.
  4. Deposit refunded. When you return all the linen, your deposit is refunded. Missing or damaged items are deducted from it.

06 — FAQ

Quick answers

Do I pay less if I book here?

Yes. Booking online on our website gets you 10% off compared with OTA prices (Booking.com, Airbnb and the other portals); booking by phone or WhatsApp gets you 5% off OTA prices. Price and terms are always shown before you confirm.

Is there parking?

Yes: for stays until 30 June 2027 parking is free, while spaces last. When you book, we'll tell you whether it's available for your dates.

How does the Riviera Rent Club work?

It's free: every stay you book direct with us earns you points (1 point for every € 10). With 100 points you get € 50 off your next holiday, or perks such as early check-in or late check-out.

What does being members of Finale Enduro mean?

We work with Finale Enduro: riders get dedicated perks, access to the Finale Enduro card network and homes equipped for bikers. Ask at our office for details.

Where do I pick up the keys?

At the info point (address under Contact), open every day 9:00–12:30 and 15:00–18:00, or directly at the property.

How much does management cost for owners?

We work on commission, only on confirmed bookings. The percentage depends on the property and the services, and we give it to you in writing after our visit.

What is the B&B Point?

It's our service for people who let on their own: linen hire, key handover with a register, cleaning and, as a separate service, paperwork. You choose what to use, with no management contract.

How does the linen deposit work?

On your first pick-up you leave a deposit, recorded in the register. It's refunded when you return all the linen; any missing or damaged items are deducted.

Which areas do you manage homes in?

All across Liguria, on the coast and inland.
