Casa Pace

8 guests · 3 bedrooms · 2 bathrooms

Private entrance and garden in Finale, 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms: ideal for families and groups.

The home

At Casa Pace, Rivierarent welcomes guests to a refined and peaceful retreat in Finale Ligure, where contemporary comfort meets the quiet charm of the Riviera. Located at Via Giovanni da Verrazzano 12, the apartment features a private entrance, a garden and lovely views—an inviting setting for a relaxing stay surrounded by the beauty of the Ligurian coast.

The thoughtfully arranged interiors offer privacy and comfort for families and groups. Just to the right of the entrance, a double bedroom includes a small kitchen and a full bathroom, providing an added sense of independence. The welcoming living room features a fully equipped kitchen and a sofa bed, making it an ideal place to unwind after a day exploring the beaches, villages and trails.

The sleeping area includes two further bedrooms: one with bunk beds and another with two single beds that can be arranged as a double. A second full bathroom serves this part of the apartment. Both bathrooms feature elegant walk-in showers.

With its inviting interiors, private entrance and garden with a view, Casa Pace is an ideal choice for guests seeking a comfortable and stylish stay in Finale Ligure, with flexible spaces and the pleasure of feeling at home.
