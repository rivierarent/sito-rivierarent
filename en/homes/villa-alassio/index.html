Apartment in a villa in Alassio surrounded by greenery, with pool and garden, close to the beaches.

The home

Welcome to our recently renovated modern apartment, located in a splendid villa in Alassio, surrounded by greenery. This elegant and comfortable space is ideal for a holiday with family or friends, with the capacity to accommodate up to 6 people. The apartment features a fully equipped kitchen, where you can prepare delicious meals, and a bright living room with a comfortable sofa bed for relaxing moments. The two bedrooms include a cozy double bedroom and a bedroom with two single beds, perfect for the younger ones. Guests will be able to take advantage of two bathrooms: one with a shower and the other with a tub/shower, ensuring maximum comfort for everyone. Outside, you will enjoy a charming garden and a swimming pool, ideal for cooling off on hot summer days. The villa is located in a strategic position, a short distance from the wonderful beaches of Alassio and local attractions. Don't miss the opportunity to spend an unforgettable holiday in this corner of paradise!
