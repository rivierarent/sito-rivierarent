Rustico del Borgo

3 guests · 2 bedrooms · 2 bathrooms

Traditional house on several levels in Ranzi, above Pietra Ligure, with a panoramic terrace over the hills.

The home

Rustico nel Borgo is a characterful home on several levels in Ranzi, in the quiet hamlet of Borgata Sant'Antonio, in the hills above Pietra Ligure.

It sleeps up to 3 people and is ideal for an authentic, relaxing stay away from the crowds, yet close to the sea. The entrance leads into the kitchen, followed by a first bathroom. Upstairs is the double bedroom with its own full bathroom, and on the second floor a small room with a single bed.

The real highlight is the panoramic terrace, furnished with a small table, perfect for breakfast outdoors, relaxing and enjoying a magnificent view over the Ligurian hills.

Perfect for couples, small families and travellers looking for an authentic village, Rustico nel Borgo combines the charm of Ligurian tradition with a welcoming, practical home. Ranzi is quiet and picturesque, ideal for walks and just a few minutes from Pietra Ligure, the sea and the main sights of the Riviera.
