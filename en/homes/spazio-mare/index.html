Spazio Mare

Elegant 75 m² two-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms, close to the sea.

The home

Welcome to Rivierarent Spazio Mare, a bright and welcoming apartment on the 4th floor in the heart of Finale Ligure, ideal for families or groups of friends looking for comfort and convenience.

Location: the apartment is on Via Torino, an exceptionally convenient spot just a few steps from the beaches, the sea and the lively seafront promenade of Finale Marina. Supermarkets, bakeries, pharmacies, bars and good restaurants are all close by, so you won't need the car for everyday shopping.

Living area: a large living room with equipped kitchen, opening onto a practical balcony.

Main bedroom: a spacious double bedroom.

Second bedroom: a comfortable, roomy bedroom with 3 single beds.

Bathrooms: 2 full bathrooms, for privacy and no waiting.
