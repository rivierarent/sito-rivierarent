The home

Rivierarent presents Villa Montebello 7B, an elegant and carefully restored home set among centuries-old olive trees on the hillside above Finale Ligure. Here, the character of a historic Italian residence meets contemporary comfort, in a peaceful and private setting with sweeping views across the Ligurian landscape to the Mediterranean.

Arranged over several levels, the villa offers distinctive and welcoming interiors. The master bedroom is especially charming, with high arched ceilings, elegant antique furnishings and a generous en-suite bathroom. Two further bedrooms provide comfortable accommodation and share a well-appointed bathroom with a shower.

Upstairs, a fully equipped kitchen and intimate dining area invite you to enjoy meals at home. The adjoining living room, furnished with comfortable seating, opens through French doors onto the terrace, bringing the Mediterranean panorama into the heart of the home. The terrace is equipped with a high-end barbecue, a shaded dining area and comfortable loungers: an inviting setting for breakfast overlooking the sea, leisurely lunches and relaxed evenings outdoors. A convenient powder room and laundry facilities complete the home. Air conditioning and heating ensure comfort throughout the seasons.

Surrounded by mature olive groves, the villa offers peace and privacy while remaining just a short walk or drive from the centre of Finale Ligure. Several restaurants are nearby, serving fresh seasonal ingredients and authentic Ligurian cuisine; farther into the hills, traditional trattorias, osterias and grill houses await.

Finale Ligure is a captivating destination where sandy beaches and the sea meet medieval villages, dramatic limestone scenery and a rich history. Discover Finalmarina, with its lively seafront, cafés and restaurants; Finalborgo, a beautifully preserved medieval village recognised among Italy’s most beautiful villages; Finalpia, home to a Benedictine monastery; and Varigotti, a picturesque former fishing village with colourful houses. The area also offers the prehistoric Arene Candide caves, ancient fortifications and the dramatic ruins of Castel Gavone.

Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy mountain biking, cycling, climbing and hiking. Finale is renowned for its enduro trails and historic routes, which cross limestone landscapes, former trading paths and military roads, with spectacular views over the Mediterranean. The property also provides a secure garage for cars and bicycles, with space and facilities to clean and store equipment after a day on the trails or roads.

Whether you begin the day with a ride through the hills, spend the afternoon at the beach, stroll through medieval Finalborgo or linger over a long lunch on the terrace, Villa Montebello 7B offers a refined and peaceful setting from which to experience the beauty and character of the Italian Riviera.
