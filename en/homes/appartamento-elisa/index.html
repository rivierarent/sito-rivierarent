Appartamento Elisa

6 guests · 2 bedrooms · 2 bathrooms · 80 m²

80 m² two-bedroom apartment with 2 bathrooms, 100 metres from the sea, for 6 guests.

The home

Welcome to Elisa, our very spacious apartment just a few steps from the sea, in the lively town centre. Its enviable location makes it ideal for a beach holiday full of relaxation and fun.

The apartment has a bright living room with two large sofas and a spacious, well-equipped kitchen area; a large bedroom with a double bed and a bunk bed, perfect for families or groups of friends; a second bedroom with a comfortable double bed; and two bathrooms, one with a shower and one with a bathtub.

There is also a balcony, perfect for a morning coffee or for unwinding after a day at the beach. The apartment is between the first and second floor and is easily reached by a few steps. Ceiling fans and mosquito nets make every stay pleasant and comfortable.

Book your dream seaside holiday and enjoy the best of our welcoming apartment!
