Il Cucchiaio – Appartamenti sul portico

4 guests · 1 bedroom · 1 bathroom · 50 m²

Bike friendly

Stone one-bedroom apartment in an inland village, among olive groves and MTB trails.

The home

Located in Orco Feglino, Il cucchiaio apartment offers an ideal location just 10 minutes drive from Finale Ligure. Access is via a flight of stairs, positioned above the Il Portico restaurant, known for its typical local cuisine and affiliated with us. For this reason, the accommodation is reserved exclusively for adults.

The apartment has a small private outdoor space, perfect for enjoying a moment of relaxation outdoors. Inside, there is a living room with a fully equipped kitchen and sofa bed, a double bedroom and a modern bathroom with shower. Everything has been recently renovated and equipped with air conditioning to ensure your comfort during your stay.

The public parking in front makes your arrival and departure easy and convenient. The combination of charm, comfort and a strategic position make it the perfect choice for a relaxing holiday and discovery of the area.
